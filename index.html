About the artist

Hello, I'm Mira.

I'm a multi-disciplinary artist based in India. My work moves between oil and watercolour, digital illustration, landscape photography and small-scale sculpture, and it keeps circling the same idea: quiet moments deserve to be looked at slowly.

I built this portfolio because social platforms compress images and bury them in a feed. Here, every piece is shown at full quality, organised into galleries, and available for commission.
